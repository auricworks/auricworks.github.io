Auric Tracker Support

If something isn't working, or you have a question or an idea, email auricworks.app@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

The app shows no steps or no heart-rate data

Auric Tracker reads everything from Apple Health. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, tap Apps, tap Auric Tracker, and make sure every type of data is turned on. Heart-rate zones and the Cardio Score come only from recorded workouts, so record your workouts with your Apple Watch or another tracker that saves workouts and detailed heart rate to Apple Health.

My streak or trends only go back 30 days

On iOS 27, Apple Health can share only the past 30 days with an app. To share your full history, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, tap Apps, tap Auric Tracker, and choose to share all recorded data.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Your iPhone counts steps on its own. Measuring training needs a heart-rate sensor, and the app works best with an Apple Watch. Another tracker works if it saves workouts and detailed heart-rate readings to Apple Health.

Moving to a new iPhone

When you move to a new iPhone with Quick Start, or restore it from an iPhone backup, all of Auric Tracker's data comes with it. If you set up the new iPhone without restoring, the app's iCloud backup brings back your goals, week settings, and achievements, and your steps and workouts come back from Apple Health. Your age, heart-rate zones, medication settings, and journal are kept only on your iPhone, so they come back only with an iPhone backup or Quick Start.

Restoring your purchase

If you bought the unlock and it isn't showing, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments and refunds. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Heart-rate medication

The heart-rate medication setting is a fitness feature, not medical advice, and Auric Tracker is not a medical device. Talk to your doctor before using heart-rate zones to guide your exercise, especially if you take medication for a heart condition.

Requirements

Auric Tracker needs an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 10 or later.
